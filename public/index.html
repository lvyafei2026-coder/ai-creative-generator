Frequently asked questions

Is this tool free?

Yes. It runs on Cloudflare Workers AI with a free daily quota. Normal use won't hit limits.

Are my photos stored?

No. Photos are processed in memory and discarded immediately after the result is generated. Nothing is stored, logged, or used for training.

Is the baby prediction accurate?

No. Baby prediction is purely for entertainment. It does not use genetics and has no medical or scientific basis. It simply blends facial features in a visually plausible way.

Why does generation take 10-30 seconds?

Image generation is computationally intensive. The model runs on Cloudflare's GPU infrastructure, and the time depends on load. Most generations complete in 10-20 seconds.

What image format works best?

Clear, well-lit photos with the face or body part clearly visible. Front-facing portraits work best for hairstyle and baby modes. Avoid blurry, low-light, or heavily filtered images.

Note: AI-generated images are for entertainment purposes only. Do not use them to make medical, cosmetic, or personal decisions.
